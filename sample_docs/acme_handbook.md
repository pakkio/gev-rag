# Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook (sample document)

## Working hours
Core hours at Acme Robotics are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they work 40 hours per week. Fridays after 14:00 are reserved as "focus time": no meetings may be scheduled.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from both your manager and the People team. Acme provides a one-time home office stipend of $600, which can be claimed within 90 days of your start date.

## Vacation and leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Carried-over days expire on March 31. Sick leave is unlimited but absences longer than three consecutive days require a doctor's note.

Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for all parents, regardless of gender, and can be taken any time within the first year after birth or adoption.

## Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the TravelDesk portal. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Meal expenses during travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Submit receipts within 30 days using the Expensify app; late submissions require VP approval.

## Equipment
Every engineer receives a laptop of their choice (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad X1) and a 27-inch monitor. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours at it-help@acme.example.

## Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences. Up to two conference days per year count as working time. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

## Security
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Never share passwords, even with IT staff. Suspected phishing emails should be forwarded to security@acme.example and then deleted. Customer data must never be stored on personal devices.
